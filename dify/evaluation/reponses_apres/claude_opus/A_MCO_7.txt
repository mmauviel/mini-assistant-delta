1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more in July: 36.2% in 2023, 34.4% in 2024, 34.6% in 2025 and 32.8% in 2026.
- In July 2026, MCO was above the Delta network average: 32.8 - 25.2 = 7.6 points.
- The cause rising most is airline: 12.8% in July against 9.1% on average (+3.7 points); late-arriving aircraft follows with 8.8% against 5.4% (+3.4 points).
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 48% and 52%.
- 60% of "national aviation system" delay minutes happened during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is recurring, because the share of delayed flights was above our 25% threshold every year, 4 years out of 4 (from 32.8% to 36.2%). Delays are mixed, since the share of delays Delta controls, between 48% and 52%, crosses 50%. Bad weather explains more than half of the air-system delay minutes, with 60% of them happening during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Reinforce standby crews and ground staff, and bring maintenance checks forward before the month – before July – airline, 12.8% in July vs 9.1% on average
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – before July – late-arriving aircraft, 8.8% in July vs 5.4% on average

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, thank you for choosing Delta. In July, arrivals at Orlando are frequently delayed, largely due to weather conditions. We recommend allowing ample time for your connections. Should your flight be delayed, you will be notified promptly in the Fly Delta app, along with the options available to you. We wish you a pleasant journey."
