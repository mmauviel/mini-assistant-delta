1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more in June: 26.5% in 2023, 20.8% in 2024, 33.8% in 2025 and 22.1% in 2026.
- In June 2026, LGA was above the Delta network average: 22.1 - 21.4 = 0.7 points.
- The cause rising most is the national aviation system: 10.0% in June against 7.8% on average (+2.2 points).
- Late-arriving aircraft: 7.7% in June against 6.6% on average (+1.1 points).
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 41% and 45%.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is one-off, because the share of delayed flights was above our 25% threshold in 2 years out of 4 (2023 and 2025), and these two years are not in a row. Delays are mostly external, since the share of delays Delta controls, between 41% and 45%, is entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the air-system delay minutes, as 48% of them happened during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier – in real time, only if delays appear in June – national aviation system, 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – in real time, only if late aircraft appear in June – late-arriving aircraft, 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to New York is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."
