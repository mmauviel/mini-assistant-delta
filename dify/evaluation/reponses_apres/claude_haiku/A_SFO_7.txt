1. Facts
- Share of Delta flights arriving at SFO delayed 15 minutes or more in July 2026: 47.3%.
- Same share in July of the other years: 23.7% (2023), 23.6% (2024), 28.1% (2025).
- Gap to the Delta network average in July (25.2%): 47.3 - 25.2 = 22.1 points.
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 42% and 46%.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is at risk, because the share was above the 25% threshold in 2025 and 2026, the last two years in a row. Delays are mostly external, since the range of 42% to 46% lies entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the air-system delay minutes, at 21%.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – before July, plus close monitoring during July – 6.4%
Suggestion 2: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier – before July, plus close monitoring during July – 42% to 46%

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, thank you for choosing Delta. In recent years, arrivals at San Francisco have frequently been delayed in July. We recommend allowing ample time for your connections. Should your flight be delayed, you will be notified promptly in the Fly Delta app. We wish you a pleasant journey."
