1. Facts

* The share of Delta flights delayed by 15 minutes or more at SFO was 47.3% in July 2026, compared with 28.1% in July 2025, 23.6% in July 2024 and 23.7% in July 2023.
* The July 2026 delay rate was 22.1 percentage points above the Delta network average (47.3 - 25.2 = 22.1 points).
* The main delay causes in July were national aviation system (13.2%), airline (10.3%), late-arriving aircraft (6.4%) and extreme weather (1.0%).
* Airline delays were 2.6 points above the airport's monthly average (10.3% vs 7.7%), while late-arriving aircraft delays were 2.1 points above average (6.4% vs 4.3%).
* Delta controls between 42% and 46% of delays. Additionally, 21% of national aviation system delay minutes occurred during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is at risk because delays exceeded the 25% threshold in the last two consecutive years, with 28.1% in 2025 and 47.3% in 2026. Delays are mostly external because Delta controls only 42% to 46% of them, a range entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of national aviation system delay minutes, accounting for 21%.
3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier – prepare before July and monitor closely during July – Delta controls 42% to 46% of delays.
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – prepare before July and monitor closely during July – late-arriving aircraft delays: 6.4% in July vs 4.3% on average.
