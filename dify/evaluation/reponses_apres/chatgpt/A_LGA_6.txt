1. Facts

* In June 2026, 22.1% of Delta flights arriving at LGA were delayed by 15 minutes or more, compared with 33.8% in June 2025 and 21.4% for the Delta network average.
* The disruption level was classified as one-off: delays exceeded the 25% threshold in 2 out of 4 years (2023 and 2025), but not in consecutive years.
* The national aviation system accounted for 10.0% of delayed flights, compared with its 7.8% average (+2.2 percentage points). It was the cause rising most.
* Late-arriving aircraft accounted for 7.7%, compared with a 6.6% average (+1.1 points). Airline-related delays accounted for 7.1%, compared with 6.3% (+0.8 points).
* Delta-controlled delays represented between 41% and 45%. Additionally, 48% of national aviation system delay minutes occurred during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is one-off because delays exceeded the 25% threshold in two out of four years, 2023 and 2025, but not in consecutive years. Delays are mostly external because the controllable share is entirely below 50%, ranging from 41% to 45%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of national aviation system delay minutes, accounting for 48%.
3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier. – In real time, only if delays appear. – National aviation system: 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average.
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew. – In real time, only if delays appear. – Late-arriving aircraft: 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average.
