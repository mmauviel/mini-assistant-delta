1. Facts

* The share of Delta flights delayed by 15 minutes or more at MCO decreased from 36.2% in July 2023 to 32.8% in July 2026.
* In July 2026, 32.8% of arrivals were delayed, compared with the Delta network average of 25.2%, a difference of 7.6 percentage points (32.8 - 25.2 = 7.6).
* Delays exceeded the 25% disruption threshold in all four years. The main causes in July were airline (12.8%), national aviation system (12.0%), and late-arriving aircraft (8.8%).
* The airline cause increased most compared with its monthly average: 12.8% versus 9.1% (+3.7 points). Late-arriving aircraft accounted for 8.8% versus 5.4% (+3.4 points).
* Between 48% and 52% of delays were controllable by Delta. Additionally, 60% of national aviation system delay minutes occurred during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is recurring because delays exceeded the 25% threshold in all four years. Delays are mixed in terms of controllability, as the estimated controllable share ranges from 48% to 52%, crossing 50%. Bad weather explains more than half of national aviation system delay minutes, accounting for 60%.
3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Reinforce standby crews and ground staff, and bring maintenance checks forward before the month. – before July – 12.8% in July versus 9.1% on average.
Suggestion 2: Track storm forecasts at arrival times and warn passengers as soon as an alert is issued. – before July – 60% of national aviation system delay minutes occurred during thunderstorms or low cloud.
