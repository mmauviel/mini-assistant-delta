1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more in June, by year: 2023 = 26.5%; 2024 = 20.8%; 2025 = 33.8%; 2026 = 22.1%.
- Disruption level: one-off (above 25% in 2 year(s) out of 4).
- National aviation system delays: 10.0% in June, against an airport average of 7.8%: 10.0 - 7.8 = 2.2 points above average. This is the cause rising most.
- Late-arriving aircraft delays: 7.7% in June, against an airport average of 6.6%: 7.7 - 6.6 = 1.1 points above average.
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 41% and 45%. Weather inside the "national aviation system" cause: 48% of its delay minutes.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is one-off, because the share of delayed flights was above our group's threshold of 25% in 2 years out of 4 (26.5% in 2023 and 33.8% in 2025), and these 2 years are not in a row. Delays are mostly external, because the share Delta controls is between 41% and 45%, a range entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the air-system delay minutes, because thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport account for 48% of them.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier – in real time, only if delays appear – national aviation system, 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – in real time, only if delays appear – late-arriving aircraft, 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to New York is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."
