1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more in February, by year: 2023 = 10.8%; 2024 = 8.8%; 2025 = 17.6%; 2026 = 11.6%.
- February 2026 compared with the Delta network average: 11.6 - 15.8 = -4.2 points.
- Disruption level: normal (above 25% in 0 year(s) out of 4).
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 56% and 60%.
- Weather inside the "national aviation system" cause: 53% of its delay minutes happened during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is normal because the share of delayed flights was never above our group's threshold of 25% in any of the 4 Februaries (the highest was 17.6% in 2025). Delays are mostly controllable, because the share Delta controls is between 56% and 60%, a range entirely above 50%. Bad weather explains more than half of the air-system delay minutes, with 53% of them happening during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Track storm forecasts at arrival times and warn passengers as soon as an alert is issued – in real time, only if delays appear – 53% of air-system delay minutes
Suggestion 2: Monitor weather alerts and prepare a targeted cancellation plan – in real time, only if delays appear

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to Atlanta is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."
