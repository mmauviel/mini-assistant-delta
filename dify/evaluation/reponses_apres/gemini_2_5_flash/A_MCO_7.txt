1. Facts

* In July, the share of Delta Air Lines mainline flights arriving at Orlando International (MCO) delayed 15 minutes or more was 36.2% in 2023, 34.4% in 2024, 34.6% in 2025, and 32.8% in 2026, compared with the July Delta network average of 25.2%.

* Airline delays in July reached 12.8% compared with the airport's all-months average of 9.1% (12.8 - 9.1 = 3.7 points), making it the cause rising most.

* National aviation system delays in July were 12.0% compared with an average of 8.7% (12.0 - 8.7 = 3.3 points), with 60% of its delay minutes occurring during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

* Late-arriving aircraft delays in July were 8.8% compared with an average of 5.4% (8.8 - 5.4 = 3.4 points), while extreme weather delays were 1.0% compared with an average of 0.5% (1.0 - 0.5 = 0.5 points).

* Tracing each late aircraft back to the flight that started the delay shows that Delta controls between 48% and 52% of delays.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level at Orlando International (MCO) in July is recurring because the share of delayed flights exceeded the 25% threshold in 4 years out of 4. The delays are mixed because the share of delays Delta controls is between 48% and 52%, which crosses the 50% mark. Bad weather explains more than half of the air-system delay minutes, accounting for 60% of the delay minutes inside the national aviation system cause.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Reinforce standby crews and ground staff, and bring maintenance checks forward before the month – before July – airline, 12.8% in July vs 9.1% on average Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – before July – late-arriving aircraft, 8.8% in July vs 5.4% on average
