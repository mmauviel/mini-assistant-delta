1. Facts

* In June, the share of Delta Air Lines mainline flights arriving at New York, NY: LaGuardia (LGA) delayed 15 minutes or more was 26.5% in 2023, 20.8% in 2024, 33.8% in 2025, and 22.1% in 2026 (compared to the Delta network average in June of 21.4%).

* National aviation system delays are the cause rising most in June at 10.0%, which is 2.2 points above the airport's all-month average of 7.8% (10.0 - 7.8 = 2.2 points).

* Late-arriving aircraft caused 7.7% of delays in June, which is 1.1 points above the airport's average of 6.6% (7.7 - 6.6 = 1.1 points), while airline delays were 7.1% (average 6.3%, +0.8 points) and extreme weather was 1.1% (average 0.6%, +0.5 points).

* Tracing each late aircraft back to the flight that started the delay shows that Delta controls between 41% and 45% of delays.

* Within the national aviation system cause, 48% of delay minutes occurred during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level at LGA in June is one-off because the share of delayed flights exceeded the 25% strong disruption threshold in 2 non-consecutive years out of 4 (26.5% in 2023 and 33.8% in 2025). The delays are mostly external because the share of delays Delta controls is between 41% and 45%, which is entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the air-system delay minutes, as thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport account for 48% of those minutes.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier (Delta does not control this cause) – in real time, only if delays appear – national aviation system, 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average. Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – in real time, only if delays appear – late-arriving aircraft, 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average.

4. Passenger message

Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding.
