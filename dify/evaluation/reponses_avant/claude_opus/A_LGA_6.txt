**1. Facts**
- Share of Delta mainline flights arriving at LGA delayed 15 minutes or more in June: 2023 = 26.5%, 2024 = 20.8%, 2025 = 33.8%, 2026 = 22.1%.
- June 2026 compared with the Delta network average in June: 22.1 - 21.4 = 0.7 points above; the highest year, 2025, was 33.8 - 21.4 = 12.4 points above.
- The share was above our 25% threshold in 2 years out of 4 (2023 and 2025).
- The national aviation system cause rose most: 10.0% in June against an average of 7.8% (10.0 - 7.8 = 2.2 points); late-arriving aircraft reached 7.7% against 6.6% (+1.1 points).
- Delta controls between 41% and 45% of delays, and 48% of the national aviation system delay minutes happened during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

**2. Interpretation**
DATA classifies the disruption at LGA in June as one-off, because the share of delayed arrivals went above our 25% threshold only in 2023 (26.5%) and 2025 (33.8%), never in two years in a row, and stayed below it in 2024 (20.8%) and 2026 (22.1%). Delays are mostly external, since the share Delta controls lies between 41% and 45%, a range entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the national aviation system delay minutes, at 48%.

**3. Suggestions**
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier (Delta does not control this cause) – in real time, only if delays appear – national aviation system at 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average.
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew – in real time, only if delays appear – late-arriving aircraft at 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average.
