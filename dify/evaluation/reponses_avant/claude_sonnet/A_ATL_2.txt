1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more at ATL in February: 2023 = 10.8%; 2024 = 8.8%; 2025 = 17.6%; 2026 = 11.6%.
- February 2026 compared with the Delta network average in February: 11.6 - 15.8 = -4.2 points.
- Late-arriving aircraft: 4.6% in February vs 6.0% on average (-1.4 points); airline: 4.2% vs 4.6% (-0.4 points).
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 56% and 60%.
- Weather accounts for 53% of the delay minutes in the national aviation system cause.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is normal: no year out of 4 was above our group's 25% threshold for a strong disruption (the highest was 17.6% in 2025). Delays are mostly controllable, because the share Delta controls is between 56% and 60%, a range entirely above 50%. Bad weather explains more than half of the air-system delay minutes, with 53% of them happening during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Track storm forecasts at arrival times and warn passengers as soon as an alert is issued – in real time, only if delays appear – 53%
Suggestion 2: Monitor weather alerts and prepare a targeted cancellation plan – in real time, only if delays appear

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to Atlanta is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."
