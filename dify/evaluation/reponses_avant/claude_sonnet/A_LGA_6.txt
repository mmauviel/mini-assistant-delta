1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more at LGA in June: 2023 = 26.5%; 2024 = 20.8%; 2025 = 33.8%; 2026 = 22.1%.
- June 2025 compared with the Delta network average in June: 33.8 - 21.4 = 12.4 points.
- National aviation system: 10.0% in June vs 7.8% on average (+2.2 points), the cause rising most.
- Late-arriving aircraft: 7.7% in June vs 6.6% on average (+1.1 points).
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 41% and 45%.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is one-off, as stated in DATA, with 2 years out of 4 above our group's 25% threshold for a strong disruption (26.5% in 2023 and 33.8% in 2025). Delays are mostly external, because the share Delta controls is between 41% and 45%, a range entirely below 50%. Bad weather does not explain more than half of the air-system delay minutes, with 48% of them happening during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier (Delta does not control this cause). – in real time, only if delays appear – 10.0%
Suggestion 2: Add buffers between rotations of aircraft passing through this airport, and track late aircraft in real time to offer a replacement aircraft or crew. – in real time, only if delays appear – 7.7%

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to New York is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."

- 10.0%, 7.8%, +2.2 points; 7.7%, 6.6%, +1.1 points: causes line.
- 41% and 45%: share of delays Delta controls.
- 48%: weather inside the national aviation system cause.
(b) Sentences that are not facts from DATA:
