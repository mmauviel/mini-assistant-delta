1. Facts
- Share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more at MCO in July: 2023 = 36.2%; 2024 = 34.4%; 2025 = 34.6%; 2026 = 32.8%.
- July 2026 compared with the Delta network average in July: 32.8 - 25.2 = 7.6 points.
- Airline: 12.8% in July vs 9.1% on average (+3.7 points), the cause rising most.
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 48% and 52%.
- Weather accounts for 60% of the delay minutes in the national aviation system cause.

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is recurring: all 4 years out of 4 were above our group's 25% threshold for a strong disruption, with the lowest at 32.8% in 2026. Delays are mixed, because the share Delta controls is between 48% and 52%, a range that crosses 50%. Bad weather explains more than half of the air-system delay minutes, with 60% of them happening during thunderstorms or low cloud at the airport.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Reinforce standby crews and ground staff, and bring maintenance checks forward before the month. – before July – 12.8%
Suggestion 2: Track storm forecasts at arrival times and warn passengers as soon as an alert is issued – before July – 60%

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, thank you for choosing Delta. In July, arrivals at Orlando are frequently delayed, largely due to weather conditions. We recommend allowing ample time for your connections. Should your flight be delayed, you will be notified promptly in the Fly Delta app, along with the options available to you. We wish you a pleasant journey."
