1. Facts
- July 2026 share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more: 32.8% (DATA line 8).
- Above the 25% threshold in 4 of 4 years, so the disruption level is recurring (DATA line 10).
- Airline cause: 12.8% against an airport average of 9.1%, +3.7 points (DATA line 12). This is the cause rising most.
- Bad weather share of national aviation system delay minutes: 60% (DATA line 18).

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is recurring, because the July share was above the 25% threshold in all 4 years, from 2023 to 2026. Delays are mixed, since Delta's controllable share ranges from 48% to 52%, which crosses 50%. Bad weather explains 60% of the national aviation system delay minutes, which is more than half.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Reinforce standby crews and ground staff, and bring maintenance checks forward before the month – before July – 12.8%
Suggestion 2: Track storm forecasts at arrival times and warn passengers as soon as an alert is issued – before July – 60%

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, thank you for choosing Delta. In July, arrivals at Orlando are frequently delayed, largely due to weather conditions. We recommend allowing ample time for your connections. Should your flight be delayed, you will be notified promptly in the Fly Delta app, along with the options available to you. We wish you a pleasant journey."
