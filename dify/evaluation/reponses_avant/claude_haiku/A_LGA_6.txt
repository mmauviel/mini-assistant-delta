1. Facts
- June 2026 share of flights delayed 15 minutes or more: 22.1% (DATA line 8).
- Delta network average in June: 21.4% (DATA line 9).
- June 2025 share: 33.8%, which is 33.8 - 21.4 = 12.4 points above the network average (DATA lines 8 and 9).
- National aviation system delays: 10.0% against an airport average of 7.8%, +2.2 points (DATA line 14). This is the cause rising most.
- Share of delays Delta controls: between 41% and 45% (DATA line 17).

2. Interpretation
The disruption level is one-off, because the June share was above the 25% threshold in 2 of the 4 years, 2023 and 2025 (DATA lines 8 and 10). Delays are mostly external, since Delta's controllable share ranges from 41% to 45%, which is entirely below 50%. Bad weather explains 48% of the national aviation system delay minutes, which is not more than half.

3. Suggestions
Suggestion 1: Prepare rebookings, coordinate with air traffic control and warn passengers earlier (Delta does not control this cause) – in real time, only if delays appear – 10.0%

4. Passenger message
"Dear Customer, we regret to inform you that your flight to New York is delayed, and we sincerely apologize for the inconvenience. Your new arrival time and the options available to you can be found in the Fly Delta app, and we will keep you informed of any further change. Thank you for your patience and understanding."
